# Park Run

Two skate games for the web, built for a multisensory interaction design module. Every move is tied to a recorded skate sound: rolling, the ollie pop, landing, rail and manual-pad slides, carving turns, and the bail.

Turn your sound on for both.

## 1. Park Run (side view)

**Play: https://pranjalru.github.io/park-run/**

An abstract runner set in a printed blue city. Jump the junk, land on rails and manual pads to grind, and throw flip tricks.

| | Laptop | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Spacebar | Tap |
| Jump (ollie) | Spacebar | Tap |
| Kickflip or shove-it | Press spacebar twice quickly | Tap twice quickly |
| Play again | Spacebar | Tap |

- Clear cans, bins, cones, boxes and rolling boards.
- Land on top of a rail or manual pad to slide along it. Hitting one from the side ends the run.
- A trick has to finish before you land, or you bail.
- **Score:** 1 point per metre, plus 10 for every flip trick you land.

## 2. Park Run Underground (top-down view)

**Play: https://pranjalru.github.io/park-run/underground/**

A top-down version in a transit-map style. You ride the centre board (the pink spiral deck) with your crew, and every skater draws a colour path behind them.

| | Laptop | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Click | Tap |
| Steer | Move the mouse left and right | Hold the phone upright and tilt it left and right |
| Kickflip or shove-it | Click | Quick tap |
| Play again | Click | Tap |

- **Stars:** you ollie over them automatically.
- **Walls:** you can't jump them. Steer through the single gap, or the run ends.
- **Rails and manual pads:** ride onto them from the end or the side to slide.
- **Score:** 1 point per metre, plus 10 for every star you jump, 10 for every rail or manual pad you get onto, and 5 for every trick.
- **Phone tilt:** on iPhone, allow motion access when Safari asks after your first tap. If tilt doesn't respond, drag a finger to steer instead.
